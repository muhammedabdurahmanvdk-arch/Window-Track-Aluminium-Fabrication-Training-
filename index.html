About Us

Window Track Aluminium Fabrication Training Centre (WindowTech) trains beginners and aspiring fabricators in the skills the aluminium window and door industry needs. Our training is practical first: students spend most of their time at the workbench, guided by experienced instructors.

Our training standards

  • Limited to 15 students per batch
  • Theory and workshop practice side by side
  • Safety, accuracy and finishing quality taught from day one
  • Support with documentation and certification formalities

Government-approved certification

On completing the course, students receive a certificate recognised by the Government of Kerala (ASAP), helping them seek employment or start their own fabrication work.
